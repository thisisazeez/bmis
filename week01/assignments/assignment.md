## Part B: Written Assignment (Practical -- 70%)

Write **300 words** addressing both of the following:

1. What is programming? Describe it as a process, not just as "writing code."
2. What are the characteristics of a good program? Name at least four characteristics and explain why each one matters.

### Requirements

- Use your own words. Definitions copied from the internet or the lecture slides will receive zero marks.
- You may reference the toothbrushing or Wi-Fi algorithm examples from class, or create your own real-world example to illustrate your points.
- Using a concrete example (your own or from class) will earn more credit than listing abstract definitions.

### Submission Format

- Typed document (Word or PDF)
- Your name and student ID at the top
- Minimum 300 words (a word count under 250 will be penalised)
